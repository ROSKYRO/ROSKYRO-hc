# /about-concierge-care

**Title:** About concierge care  
**Meta title:** About Concierge Care in India | ROSKYRO  
**Meta description:** ROSKYRO offers concierge care in India: a personal physician, same-day visits, a 24/7 direct line and hospital advocacy for individuals, couples and families.

**Body (markdown):**

````markdown
ROSKYRO is a healthcare concierge practice in India. Members pay an annual membership. In return they get a personal physician who keeps a small panel, answers the phone, sees them the same day and stays with them if they are admitted to hospital.

Concierge care, also called membership medicine or retainer medicine, exists because the usual arrangement has stopped working for many families. The OPD visit is short, the doctor changes, and nobody holds the whole story. We built a practice around the opposite idea: fewer patients per physician, and more time for each one.

## What concierge care at ROSKYRO includes

- **Same-day visits.** Write before noon and you are seen the same day. Evening slots exist for people who work.
- **A number that answers.** Your physician's phone, not a call centre. Nights and travel included.
- **Unhurried appointments.** The panel is small, so visits start on time and are not squeezed into twelve minutes.
- **Hospital advocacy.** If you are admitted, your physician is in the room with your notes and with your family.
- **One chart, kept for years.** Medicines, allergies, what you already tried and what you will not do again.
- **Care that travels.** Video visits from a hotel, a second opinion before surgery, records that follow you to another city or country.

## Everyday medicine, handled properly

Your personal physician is your first call, not your last resort. Membership covers the full range of ongoing primary care:

- A long annual health review with full labs and a written plan you can actually keep
- Acute illness such as fever, injury or a night that went wrong
- Long-term conditions including diabetes, blood pressure, heart disease and thyroid problems, managed without the ping-pong of referrals
- Medicine reconciliation: every pill and supplement checked against the others
- Family care for parents, children and the relative who never books an appointment
- Second opinions before a surgery or scan that does not sit right

You can keep your existing specialists. Your physician coordinates them, so you have one person who sees the whole picture. See how this works at every stage in our [360° care](/360-care) journey.

## How a ROSKYRO membership works

Choose a physician in your city, or ask us to suggest one. You then have a conversation about your health, your family and what you expect from a doctor. Membership begins only after that conversation and a written agreement. Fees are quoted individually, because cities, physicians and households differ.

There are three formats:

- **Individual.** One adult, with unlimited visits, 24/7 physician access, same-day appointments, hospital advocacy and a written annual plan.
- **Couple.** Two adults who share a household. Joint records when you want them, private notes when you do not.
- **Household.** Two adults and children under 18, or a parent you look after in another city, with one physician leading coordinated care.

Each physician keeps a deliberately limited panel, often between 50 and 150 families. That is why we sometimes have to say a panel is full.

## Concierge care is not health insurance

Membership pays for your physician's time and access. It does not replace health insurance. Keep your existing cover for hospital admissions, scans and hospital bills. What changes is who is looking after you before, during and after those events.

## Where we practise

ROSKYRO physicians practise in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Hyderabad and Chennai. Some of our members live abroad and rely on us for parents who live here. We work with local hospitals and keep a record that a family member overseas can trust.

## Who concierge care suits

Concierge care suits busy professionals who cannot lose a morning to a waiting room, families looking after ageing parents, people with long-term conditions who want continuity, and households that are tired of carrying three sets of notes between three doctors.

It is not for emergencies. In an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital, and call us as soon as you can.
````

# /what-is-concierge-medicine

**Title:** What is concierge medicine?  
**Meta title:** What Is Concierge Medicine? A Guide for Physicians | ROSKYRO  
**Meta description:** Concierge medicine, or membership medicine, swaps the twelve-minute OPD for a small panel. See how it works in India and how it differs from insurance.

**Body (markdown):**

````markdown
Concierge medicine is a model of care in which patients pay an annual membership fee to a physician who keeps a small panel. In return, patients get longer visits, same-day access and a direct line to their doctor. It is also called membership medicine or retainer medicine.

The idea began in the United States in the 1990s. In India it is newer, and it fits a real gap: private patients who can afford a long relationship with a doctor but cannot find one, and physicians who would like to practise that way but have no operations behind them.

## How concierge medicine works

Patients pay an annual fee. The physician stops running volume. A panel of a few dozen to a few hundred families replaces a daily OPD of many times that. Visits lengthen, phone access becomes real, and hospital work becomes part of the job again instead of an unpaid extra.

At ROSKYRO, panels are often 50 to 150 families, depending on specialty and on how the physician likes to work. We do not let a panel grow past what the physician can know.

## What members receive

- Same-day appointments when they are unwell
- A physician's direct phone number, including nights and travel
- Long visits that start on time
- Presence and advocacy if they are admitted to hospital
- One long chart held by one physician
- Coordination of any specialists they already see

## Concierge medicine is not insurance

This is the most common misunderstanding. Membership does not pay for admissions, scans or procedures. Members continue to use their existing health insurance for those. The membership pays for the physician's time and availability.

## How it differs from a conventional OPD

In a conventional OPD, a physician may see a large number of patients a day, and each visit is short. The patient may see a different doctor each time. In a concierge practice, the numbers are reversed. The physician sees fewer people, knows each one, and is paid for access and continuity rather than for the count of consultations.

## What it means for a physician

Physicians who move to a membership panel usually keep their hospital attachments and admitting rights. The OPD volume is what changes. They own the patient relationship. The operations, which means membership billing, patient communication, appointment intake and a public profile, can be run by a partner such as ROSKYRO so that the physician practises rather than manages.

It is real work. The transition takes time, and it is easy to underestimate the conversation with patients you cannot take on. We plan for about a hundred days. Faster is usually a sign that the hard conversations were skipped.

## Is concierge medicine right for you?

For patients, it suits people who value continuity and access and who are willing to pay for them. For physicians, it suits those who want the medical relationship back and who can accept a different income pattern in exchange for a practice they can control.

## Learn more

Read [why concierge medicine](/why-concierge-medicine) works for physicians, see the [affiliate benefits](/affiliate-benefits), look at the [transition plan](/transition) or browse the [physician FAQ](/physician-faq). If you are a patient, start with [about concierge care](/about-concierge-care).
````

# /why-concierge-medicine

**Title:** Why concierge medicine  
**Meta title:** Why Concierge Medicine? Why Physicians Switch | ROSKYRO  
**Meta description:** Why physicians in India leave volume practice for a membership panel, what they gain, what they give up, and who concierge medicine is not for.

**Body (markdown):**

````markdown
Because the current bargain is dishonest. Patients pretend a twelve-minute visit was enough. Physicians pretend they remember the last visit. Hospitals pretend a duty doctor is continuity.

Concierge practice is for physicians who want the medical relationship back, and who can accept a smaller, steadier practice in exchange for a working life that is still a profession.

## Why physicians choose concierge medicine

- **Time with each patient.** A visit can take as long as the problem takes.
- **Knowing your patients.** With a panel small enough to know, you remember the story, not just the chart.
- **Continuity in hospital.** Admissions become part of the relationship again. You are the doctor in the room, not a name on a referral letter.
- **A calmer calendar.** Fewer patients, predictable on-call patterns, and room for a life outside the clinic.
- **Independence.** You decide who joins your panel and how you practise.
- **Honest ethics.** Closed panels are part of the model. Patients you cannot serve well are referred honestly, not squeezed in.

## Why patients choose it

Patients come for access: a same-day visit, a number that answers, a physician who reads the labs before they have to ask. They stay for continuity. One doctor reconciles the nine medicines that three specialists stacked, notices a trend months before it becomes a crisis, and shows up when a parent is admitted in another city.

## What you give up

Concierge medicine is not an easy exit from hard work, and it is not passive income. You give up the volume of a busy OPD. Your income pattern will change, and the transition year asks for real effort. You will also be reachable by your members in a way that a conventional practice never required. We would rather say this plainly than sell you a fantasy.

## Who concierge medicine is not for

It is not for anyone chasing a lifestyle brand. It is not for physicians who want to hand the clinical relationship to someone else. And it is not for those who want growth at any cost. At ROSKYRO, scale is the enemy of the product. The work is still medicine. It is simply allowed to take the time it takes.

## How ROSKYRO helps

ROSKYRO provides the operations layer: membership billing, patient communication, a public profile, appointment intake, on-call design and existing hospital relationships. You keep clinical independence. We keep the spreadsheet.

## Next steps

Start with [what is concierge medicine](/what-is-concierge-medicine), read the [affiliate benefits](/affiliate-benefits), see how the [transition](/transition) works, or hear from physicians who have already moved in [success stories](/success-stories). When you are ready, [become an affiliate](/become-an-affiliate).
````

**Title:** 360° care  
**Meta title:** 360° Care: Appointments to Hospital Support | ROSKYRO  
**Meta description:** ROSKYRO's 360° care covers your whole treatment journey: appointments, tests, specialists, ambulance coordination, hospital support and long-term follow-up.

**Body (markdown):**

````markdown
360° care means one team stays responsible for you from the first request to recovery and beyond. Most healthcare in India is a set of separate encounters: a clinic here, a lab there, a hospital somewhere else, and you in the middle carrying the file. At ROSKYRO your personal physician and a care coordinator hold the whole journey, so you do not have to.

It covers appointments, treatment, tests, specialists, medicines, emergencies and ambulance coordination, hospital admission, follow-up and the long relationship that sits behind all of it.

## Who does what

- **Your personal physician** makes the clinical decisions. They examine you, diagnose, treat, decide which tests and specialists you need, and own your health plan.
- **Your care coordinator** handles the logistics: appointments, records, test bookings, transport, admission paperwork and the file that has to be there before you are.
- **The ROSKYRO practice** runs memberships, keeps your records safe and makes sure someone always picks up.
- **You** tell us what is happening and make the final decisions. Nothing happens without your agreement.

## Stage 1: Appointment and access

Write to us before noon and you are seen the same day. You can ask through the [find a doctor](/find-a-doctor) form, by phone or on WhatsApp. We save your request, then open WhatsApp to the clinic with the note already written, so you do not have to explain twice. Evening slots exist for people who work, and visits can be in person or by video when you are travelling.

## Stage 2: The first conversation and your plan

The first visit is long. Your physician takes your full history, reviews old reports, orders the right labs and then gives you a written plan you can actually keep. Your coordinator collects records from earlier doctors and hospitals, so the story starts complete.

## Stage 3: Consultation and treatment

Your physician treats what they can treat: fever, injury, infections, and long-term conditions such as diabetes, blood pressure, thyroid and heart disease. Every medicine and supplement is checked against the others, which is quiet work that prevents harm. Where it helps, we bring in a dietitian ROSKYRO trusts. You deal with one doctor who remembers you, not a new face at each visit.

## Stage 4: Tests and diagnostics coordination

Your physician decides which tests you need, and just as importantly which ones you do not. Your coordinator then arranges the booking, makes sure the laboratory or scan centre has the right request, and sends records ahead. Your physician reads the results before you have to ask, and explains them in plain language. A scan that does not sit right gets a second look. A scan you do not need does not get ordered.

## Stage 5: Specialists and second opinions

When you need a specialist, your physician chooses one for your problem, hands over your history and stays in the loop. After the consultation, the findings come back to your physician, who explains what they mean and how they fit with the rest of your care. Before any surgery, you can ask for a second opinion from another doctor you trust. You keep your own specialists if you already have them. We coordinate them.

## Stage 6: Emergencies and ambulance coordination

Your physician's number answers at night and while you travel. If you are unwell and unsure, call us first. If it is a life-threatening emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital straight away, and call us on the way.

We help coordinate the ambulance, advise on which hospital is the right one for the problem, tell the receiving team what they need to know and stay on the phone with your family. For a child's emergency, the nearest children's hospital comes first, with us on the call. ROSKYRO is not an emergency ambulance service, but we make sure you are not working it out alone.

## Stage 7: Hospital admission and advocacy

If you are admitted, your coordinator handles the paperwork and makes sure your records and medicine list reach the hospital before you do. Your physician is in the room with your notes and with your family. They speak to the treating team, ask the questions you would not think to ask and explain decisions in language everyone understands. Keep your health insurance for the admission itself. Our job is to make sure you are not facing it as a stranger.

## Stage 8: Discharge, recovery and follow-up

Leaving hospital is a risky moment. Your physician reviews the discharge summary, reconciles your medicines again, arranges follow-up tests and visits, and checks in on how you are recovering. If you need physiotherapy, nursing or other recovery support, we help you find the right people and coordinate with them.

## Stage 9: The relationship, year after year

Everything above goes into one chart that is allowed to be long: medicines, allergies, what you tried, what you will not do again. Each year your physician reviews it with you and updates your written plan. The relationship runs through:

- **Your whole household.** Parents, children and the relative who never books an appointment, in one coordinated record under a household membership.
- **Care that travels.** Video visits from a hotel, records that follow you to another city or country.
- **Family abroad.** If your parents live in India and you do not, we keep you informed with their consent, and we are the adult in the room when you cannot be.
- **Privacy.** We share your information with family only as you allow, and never sell it. See our [privacy policy](/privacy).

## What 360° care is not

It is not health insurance. Hospital, laboratory, scan, ambulance and specialist charges are billed by those providers and are separate from your membership, which pays for your physician's time and our coordination. It is also not a replacement for emergency services or for hospital care. It is the thing that makes both work better.

## How is this different from a normal clinic?

A normal clinic treats the visit. We look after the person across the year: the booking, the tests, the specialist, the night call, the admission and the follow-up. One physician, one coordinator and one record stay with you throughout.
````
